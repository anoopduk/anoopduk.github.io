About the meeting

Ideas move further when disciplines meet.

CM4 2026 brings together researchers working on computational chemistry, molecular and materials modelling, bonding, reactivity, catalysis, biomolecular systems, and data-driven approaches in molecular science.

The programme will include invited lectures, thematic sessions, academic discussion and time for informal interaction in a quiet residential setting.
