My teaching spans computational chemistry, scientific computing, data science and introductory computer science. The subjects differ, but the underlying aim is consistent: students should be able to move from a question to a defensible model, inspect the evidence and explain the limits of their conclusion.

## How I approach a course

I combine conceptual explanation with guided practice. Students work with real or realistic data, make explicit choices, document their reasoning and learn to distinguish a technically successful output from a scientifically meaningful result.

## Current interests

- Computational and theoretical chemistry
- Data and AI methods for scientists
- Scientific programming and reproducible research
- Project-based and studio-based learning
- Assessment that tests reasoning rather than recall alone
